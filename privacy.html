Cora Privacy Policy

Cora: Phone Cleaner & Storage · Effective 11 October 2026

The short version. Cora works on your phone. Your photos, videos, files, contacts and app information are read on your phone and never uploaded to us. We run no server and have no account system. The only data that leaves your phone goes directly to Google, for ads (Google AdMob), purchases (Google Play), and the optional features described below.

1. Who we are

Cora ("Cora", "we", "us") is a storage and video tool for Android, published on Google Play by the developer named on its Google Play listing. For any privacy question, email friendlink00@gmail.com.

2. What Cora reads on your phone, and why

To do its job, Cora reads the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us, because we have no server to send it to.

3. What leaves your phone

Ads — Google AdMob

The free version can show a rewarded video ad, only when you tap "Watch an ad" to unlock something. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Nothing about ads runs before you tap. When an ad is requested, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first through Google's consent form. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. Cora Premium shows no ads.

Purchases — Google Play

Cora Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; Cora never sees your card, bank or wallet details. Cora receives only whether you own Premium (and which plan), so it can unlock it. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

Optional: Google Play version check

Off by default. If you turn it on in App storage, Cora asks Google Play for the current version of the apps on your phone, to tell you which have updates. This sends the names (package names) of those apps to Google Play. It never sends them to us.

Optional: Shizuku

If you install the separate Shizuku app and allow Cora to use it, Cora can measure hidden app folders and clear app caches. This happens on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

4. Gmail (when this feature is available)

A future version of Cora may let you connect a Gmail account to see what fills your mailbox and clean it up. If and when you choose to connect one:

  • Cora reads message metadata only — sender, subject, date, labels, size and the unsubscribe header — never the body of your emails.
  • Moving mail to Trash, unsubscribing or taking mail out of Spam happens only when you confirm it, one action at a time. Cora cannot permanently delete email.
  • Your Gmail data is processed on your phone and stored only in Cora's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, never sold, and never used for advertising. No ads are shown on email screens, and no email data is shared with the ad network.
  • Cora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time in your Google Account permissions; uninstalling Cora deletes all mail data it stored.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run a server, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, files, contacts or email.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • Cora never removes, moves or changes a file without your confirmation. Removed files go to your phone's bin first, where they can be restored until it empties.

6. Keeping data safe

Data Cora keeps is stored in its private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to Google (ads, purchases, the optional checks and Gmail) are encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's Settings; the related feature then stops.
  • You can delete everything Cora stored by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.
  • Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data held by Google (ads, purchases), use your Google Account.
  • Questions: friendlink00@gmail.com.

8. Children

Cora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
