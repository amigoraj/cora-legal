Cora Privacy Policy

Cora: Phone Cleaner & Storage · Effective 10 November 2026 (version 1.2)

The short version. Cora works on your phone. Your photos, videos, files, contacts and app information are read on your phone and never uploaded. We run no server, have no account system and show no ads. Cora connects only to Google Play (when you buy or restore Premium, or if you turn on the optional version check) and to Cloudflare's public speed test, only when you tap "Test speed".

1. Who we are

Cora ("Cora", "we", "us") is a storage and video tool for Android, published on Google Play by the developer named on its Google Play listing. For any privacy question, email friendlink00@gmail.com.

Everything Cora works out — reports, history, fingerprints of photos and videos used to find copies — is stored in Cora's private storage on your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall Cora or clear its data.

What Cora may change, only if you allow it. During a Cool-down, Cora can lower the screen brightness and shorten the screen timeout (Modify system settings), and puts them back afterwards. To help you put apps to sleep, Cora opens Android's own settings pages and can show a short guide over them (Display over other apps). Cora cannot change those settings itself: you tap them.

3. What leaves your phone

Cora shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

Purchases — Google Play

Cora Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; Cora never sees your card, bank or wallet details. Cora receives only whether you own Premium (and which plan), so it can unlock it. If you start a free trial, Cora keeps the date it ends on your phone, to show a countdown and remind you two days before. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

Optional: Google Play version check (Premium)

Off by default. If you turn it on in App storage, Cora asks Google Play for the current version of the apps on your phone, to tell you which have updates. This sends the names (package names) of those apps to Google Play. It never sends them to us.

Speed test — Cloudflare (only when you tap)

In Network Doctor, "Test speed" downloads from and uploads random data to Cloudflare's public speed test (speed.cloudflare.com) for a few seconds. As with any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address. No personal data, files or identifiers are sent, and nothing runs unless you tap. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

4. Gmail (when this feature is available)

A future version of Cora may let you connect a Gmail account to see what fills your mailbox and clean it up. If and when you choose to connect one:

  • Cora reads message metadata only — sender, subject, date, labels, size and the unsubscribe header — never the body of your emails.
  • Moving mail to Trash, unsubscribing or taking mail out of Spam happens only when you confirm it, one action at a time. Cora cannot permanently delete email.
  • Your Gmail data is processed on your phone and stored only in Cora's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, never sold, and never used for advertising.
  • Cora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time in your Google Account permissions; uninstalling Cora deletes all mail data it stored.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run a server, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, files, contacts or email.
  • We do not show ads, and Cora contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • Cora never removes, moves or changes a file without your confirmation. Removed files go to your phone's bin first, where they can be restored until it empties.

6. Keeping data safe

Data Cora keeps is stored in its private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to Google (purchases, and Gmail when available) are encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's Settings; the related feature then stops.
  • You can delete everything Cora stored by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.
  • Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data held by Google about your purchases, use your Google Account.

8. Children

Cora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
